# Candidate tips and why they lost

Every candidate is 1–200 chars, UTF-8, single line, no trailing whitespace.

1. **`Retrying, like you, on the thing that already failed. It'll work this time.`** — WINNER. 75 chars.
   Two audiences in one clause ("Retrying"), one pivot ("it'll work this time"),
   no filler. Reads as both CI rerun and agent retry loop.

2. `Still thinking. Which, statistically, means it may be confidently wrong.` — 74 chars.
   Strong on the AI-agent side, thinner on the developer side. Runner-up.

3. `Warming up. Unlike your context window, this one never forgets.` — 65 chars.
   Shorter, but leans harder on agent jargon ("context window") than
   developer pain. Slightly narrow.

4. `Loading. If this is a loop, at least you're not alone in it.` — 57 chars.
   Shortest but soft: the "loop" word is doing all the work and the
   landing is mild. Wins on economy, loses on laugh.

5. `Thinking... burning tokens so you don't have to. You're welcome.` — 60 chars.
   Closest to a plain coffee-joke substitute ("someone else is paying"),
   no real pivot. Kept as baseline.
